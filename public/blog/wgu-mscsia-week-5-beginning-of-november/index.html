WGU MSCSIA Week 5: Beginning of November

Nov 5, 2024

It’s the beginning of November and I just submitted my paper for D486! I’m about 50/50 on it and I’m sure I’ll get it returned to fix something only because the rubric for was very ambiguous. I think I know what they want but I will see what happens after they review it.

I’m pulling through D482 especially the certmaster lessons. I been going through them on my spare time at work and work on the practice quizzes when I can. The labs are pretty good as they provide hands on experience with tools and software that I normally don’t use at work. There is a paper assignment for this course as well but it doesn’t look too bad. It looks like you fill out the a form regarding the lab you took. I only just saw a glimpse of it so I’m not 100% sure until I actually tackle it. I’m hoping to take the CySa+ exam before Thanksgiving (Or earlier!) and get the next course started by the end of November too. (Or early December!)

I did sign up for Game Off 2024 and I want to use twine again but I don’t think I’ll have time for it. I want to get into circuit boarding and hardware programming since I saw a arduino kit being offered for free by inventr.io so I might get into that as well.

See you guys next week.
