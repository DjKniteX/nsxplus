WGU MSCSIA - Week 7: CySA+ Time

Nov 19, 2024

Just wrapped up Week 7 and finished the Paper Assignment. It wasn’t that bad. I was expecting an essay but it was pretty straightforward. It did got sent back because I misread one of the columns. The virtual lab that you had to do for the Paper Assignment was a bit confusing cause it doesn’t label what you need to go to so you’ll have to do some cyber sleuthing which I believe is a skill that everyone should master.

Now that the PA is out of the way, it’s mostly CySA+ Studying which I have been doing some reading before bed and will probably do the PBQs during work and work on the labs during the evening. I’m hoping to try and start Pentest+ Course in December and take the exam for that in January. Would like to finish the next 2 classes in January. Not sure if I can finish this term (March) but I would like to only need to take 3 classes for the next term. I will be on track to finish this in a year but I don’t want to take 6 months to finish the next few courses and spend almost 5k doing it. (Though the student perks do make up for it)

Also started Game Off 2024. I will be using Twine/Sugarcube to make another text game that will be adapted from a short story I wrote in College (Which I went back and re-read and it was pretty cringe). Which in turn was adapted from a dream I had. I decided to go the text adventure route because it would be quick and easy for to whip something up and finally start writing out my Mythos/Shared Universe

Until next time.
