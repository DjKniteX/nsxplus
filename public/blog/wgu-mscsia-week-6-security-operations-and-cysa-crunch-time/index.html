WGU MSCSIA Week 6: Security Operations and CySA+ Crunch Time

Nov 12, 2024

Another week has gone by and I was able to finish D486 GRC first try! No comment from me except to just follow the rubric. I am not trying to finish a class in a day or in a week because I would like to retain the information I’m learning. When I started this paper, I would try to do a section per day (at least outlining) and then when I feel like it’s good to go, then I have a go at it and write to my heart’s content.

Now this week I will be starting the paper for D483 and be studying for the Comptia CySA+ exam. I’ll continue to watch movies in the background and work on the PBQs on certmaster during my down time at work. I’m hoping to schedule the test before December and then on the week of I will work through practice exams and studying what I got wrong.

I don’t think I’ll have time to work on my project for Game Off 2024. I am contributing on QAing open source engines so maybe I’ll start on my games again.

I did see Sunami and Big Boy this weekend so it was filled with hardcore music. This is unrelated but I just wanted to add that I’m not always studying and have been seeing concerts as of late. When I went to buy merch at the Monster Outbreak tour in San Jose, I bought an Impurity and Killing Time shirt. I lost my Killing Time shirt but I still got the pin. Sucks though. But I’m always down to rep more hardcore bands.
