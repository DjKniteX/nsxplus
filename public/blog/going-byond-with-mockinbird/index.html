Going Byond with Mockinbird

Jun 11, 2025

Some team news for mockinbird.dev since its been awhile but I’m going to pivot the team to a small indie team that works/build games using old tech (and maybe some new depending on the project) such as Byond.

I’m still going to have the main focus for mockinbird.dev to be a game jam team so we will be working mostly with RPG Maker and Byond.

Our Game Jam tech will now either be RPG Maker or Pico for quick development and used as framework for future projects.

I am planning on using our Game Jam game for 2025 to be a first of an RPG Maker Series based in the same world as Homeward Bound and Moonward Bound.
