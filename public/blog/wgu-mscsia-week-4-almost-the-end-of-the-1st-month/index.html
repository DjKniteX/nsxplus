WGU MSCSIA Week 4: Almost the end of the 1st month.

Oct 29, 2024

It’s almost the end of October and I’m about to wrap up my 2nd class. I’m still working through the PA and I’m hoping to get it done by October 31st. I started it last week but took a couple days break to hit up some concerts in Los Angeles and San Jose.

This paper had more research I needed to do but once I got the materials I needed, it’ll be a breeze! (I needed to look at impact rating standards). I also have to find sources supporting the rating and how to deal with them as well for D486. But now that I know how the assessments are done, my tip is to write with the rubric. If it tells you to list 3 things, list those 3 things. No less and no more.

Also this week I will start on the course for Security Operations. With the head start I already taken for it (CySa+ prepping videos). I think I might be able to take the comptia exam before Thanksgiving and start on my next set of classes before December as well.

Not much of an update for this week, but I’ll let you guys know how the CySA+ course goes. I believe is both a paper assessment and a comptia exam. (Let’s get my next cert!)

The goal is to try and get to the capstone as my last class on the final month of the term.
